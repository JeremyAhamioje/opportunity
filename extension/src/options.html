Browser capture

Where conversations are sent, and the token that authorises them.

The address you open the app at. No trailing slash needed.

Generate this in the app under Settings → Browser capture. It is shown once. It is not your password, and it can be revoked there at any time.

What this extension does

  • Reads the conversation text rendered on a supported page, when you click Save.
  • Sends it to the address above with your device token.
  • Never reads cookies, credentials, or MFA codes.
  • Never bypasses any site's authentication, and captures nothing on its own.
